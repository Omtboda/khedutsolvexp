## About the Company
Khedut Solvexp Pvt. Ltd. is an Indian manufacturer and supplier of Oil Cakes, edible oils and high-protein extraction meals and exporters of edible oils and extraction meals. The company is located in Gondal City of Rajkot District, Gujarat. For over three decades, we have connected farms, to produce food and feeds, add to the value chains with uncompromising principles: nutrition and trust. We specialize in rapeseed/mustard, groundnut, and soybean derivatives, operating with food-safety discipline, transparent trade, and a deep respect for growers and buyers alike.

## Manufacturing Processes

### Groundnut Seed Process (PDF pages 12–13)
1. **Pre-cleaning / De-stoning:** Initial preparation of groundnuts.
2. **Conditioning:** Adjusting moisture in groundnuts.
3. **Oil Mill (Expellers / Pre-press):** Extracting oil from groundnuts.
4. **Outputs:** Crude Groundnut Oil + Groundnut Cake; described as initial products of oil extraction.
5. **Cake to Solvent Extraction:** Processing cake to obtain DOC meal.
6. **Crude Oil to Refinery:** Refining crude oil into refined oil.
7. **Refined Groundnut Oil:** Product of the process.

The PDF includes the statement: “At Every Product Stage we are ready to satisfy the needs of our consumers.”

**Factory aerial image labels on page 13:** DOC Storage; Oil Mill 1; Oil Mill 2; Raw Material Storage; Oil Cake Storage; Oil Tank Storage; Refinery.

### Oil Mill — Crude Oil Process (PDF page 14)
1. **Cleaning & Conditioning:** Initial preparation of seed for pressing.
2. **Expeller Pressing / Pre-press:** Extraction of oil from raw materials.
3. **Triple-Filter Clarification:** Removal of fines and gums for cleaner crude oil.
4. **Crude Oil Day Tanks to Main Tank Farm:** Transfer of oil to storage facilities.
5. **Dispatch:** Oil ready for dispatch.

### Oil Mill — Oil Cake Process (PDF page 14)
1. **Cleaning & Conditioning:** Initial preparation of seed for pressing.
2. **Expeller Pressing / Pre-press:** Solids separated as oil cake.
3. **Cooling / De-dusting (Condensation):** Temperature drop and dust control for safe handling.
4. **Packing:** Bagging/tracking to specification (wording in the PDF diagram).
5. **Dispatch: Oil Cake:** Ready for dispatch.

### Solvent Extraction — De-oiled Cake (PDF page 16)
1. **Hexane Wash:** Extracts remaining oil.
2. **Separation:** Divides into meal and oil-hexane.
3. **Heating Process:** Removes residual solvent.
4. **Cooling Process:** Stabilizes meal temperature.
5. **Packaging:** Packs meal for dispatch.
6. **Dispatch.**

### Solvent Extraction — Solvent-Extracted Oil (PDF page 16)
1. **Hexane Wash:** Oil cake treated with hexane.
2. **Mixture Separation:** Mixture separated into streams.
3. **Hexane Evaporation:** Hexane separated and recovered.
4. **Crude Oil Transfer:** Oil moves to storage tanks.
5. **Dispatch.**

### Edible Oil Refinery (PDF pages 18–19)
The two diagrams together show these steps:
1. **Raw Oil Input / Reception:** Oil drawn from storage tanks; unrefined oils received from extraction.
2. **Caustic & Phosphoric Acid / Chemical Addition:** Acids and caustic chemicals are added; impurities are neutralized.
3. **Alpha Level Separator / Separation:** Sludge, sediments and impurities are removed (quality check).
4. **Vacuum Dryer:** Moisture and volatiles are eliminated.
5. **Bleacher:** Color and residual impurities are removed.
6. **Filter:** Clarity and stability are checked.
7. **Deodorization:** Odors and unwanted volatiles are stripped.
8. **Cooling Process:** Oil temperature is stabilized.
9. **Refined Oil Tank:** Final purity and color check (quality check).
10. **Dispatch:** Oil is ready for delivery.
11. **Sludge Collection:** Waste stream is collected for disposal.
