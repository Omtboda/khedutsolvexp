About Us

Three Decades of Excellence

Khedut Solvexp Pvt. Ltd. is an Indian manufacturer and supplier of Oil Cakes, edible oils and high-protein extraction meals and exporters of edible oils and extraction meals. The company is located in Gondal City of Rajkot District, Gujarat.

For over three decades, we have connected farms, to produce food and feeds, add to the value chains with uncompromising principles: nutrition and trust. We specialize in rapeseed/mustard, groundnut, and soybean derivatives, operating with food-safety discipline, transparent trade, and a deep respect for growers and buyers alike.
